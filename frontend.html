About Road Damage

Road damage such as potholes and cracks develops because of heavy traffic, water seepage, poor construction quality, and changing weather conditions. Undetected road damage increases the risk of accidents, vehicle damage, traffic congestion, and expensive maintenance. Using AI and computer vision, road damage can be detected automatically from images, making inspection faster, more accurate, and cost-effective.

Common Causes

  • Heavy traffic load
  • Rainwater and poor drainage
  • Temperature changes
  • Poor construction materials
  • Lack of maintenance
  • Natural wear and tear
